# ALote 🇲🇲

**ALote** is a Myanmar-focused job discovery platform connecting job seekers with employers across Remote, Hybrid, and On-site opportunities.

> ALote သည် မြန်မာနိုင်ငံရှိ အလုပ်ရှာဖွေသူများနှင့် အလုပ်ရှင်များကို ပိုမိုလွယ်ကူစွာ ချိတ်ဆက်ပေးရန် ရည်ရွယ်ထားသော Job Discovery Platform ဖြစ်ပါသည်။

## About ALote

ALote is built to make job discovery simple and accessible.

Job seekers can explore available opportunities, view job information, and apply for suitable positions.

Employers can submit job opportunities to ALote for review before they are published on the platform.

## Current Features

### For Job Seekers
- Browse job opportunities
- Search and filter jobs
- Browse jobs by category
- View job details
- Find Remote, Hybrid, and On-site opportunities
- Apply for available positions

### For Employers
- Submit job posting requests
- Provide company and job information
- Add salary, requirements, and responsibilities
- Select job category and work style
- Set application deadlines

### Platform Management
- Review job submissions
- Approve and publish jobs
- Manage published jobs
- Review job applications
